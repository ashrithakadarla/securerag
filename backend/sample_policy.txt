Company Leave Policy

Employees are entitled to 12 casual leaves per calendar year. Casual leave cannot be carried forward to the next year and must be applied for at least one day in advance, except in emergencies.

Sick leave is granted up to 10 days per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days. Unused sick leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 15 days.

Earned leave accrues at the rate of 1.5 days per month of service, capping at 30 days per year. Employees must submit earned leave requests through the HR portal at least one week in advance.

Maternity leave is provided for 26 weeks as per company policy, in line with statutory requirements. Paternity leave is granted for 2 weeks.

Employees on probation are not entitled to casual or earned leave during the first 3 months of employment, though sick leave may be granted on a case-by-case basis.
